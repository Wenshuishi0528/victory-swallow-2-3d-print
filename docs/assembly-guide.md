# Assembly Guide

This model is color-separated and split for FDM printing. The exact assembly order can vary by printer, support cleanup, and personal preference.

## Basic Workflow

1. Remove supports carefully.
2. Clean all contact surfaces and tabs.
3. Dry-fit every part before using glue.
4. Assemble large body sections first.
5. Add smaller wings, fins, and detail parts after the main body alignment is stable.
6. Use a small amount of glue and keep pressure even while curing.

## Tips

- Do not force thin parts into place. Sand or trim small interference points instead.
- Check left/right symmetry before gluing mirrored parts.
- Keep white and yellow visible panels clean during glue-up.
- If a part warps, test-fit it against the matching body section before deciding whether to reprint.

## Adhesive

For PLA, common options include cyanoacrylate glue, model cement compatible with your material, or a small amount of two-part epoxy for stronger joints. Test on scrap material first if appearance matters.

## Safety

Use glue, sanding tools, and blades with proper ventilation and care.
